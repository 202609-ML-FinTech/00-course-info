CV error against degree, state the chosen degree, then compare with the ANOVA sequence of
nested fits. Do the two tools agree? One sentence on why they can disagree.

**(b)** A step function of `age` with the number of cuts chosen by cross-validation. Plot the
fit on the data.

Finish with **one paragraph**: a bank wants to use age to set a salary expectation for a
mortgage. Which of your two fits would you give them, and what is one way each could mislead?
