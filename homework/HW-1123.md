## Q8 · Subset selection and the lasso on simulated data

**(a)–(b)** Generate X and ε, then Y from the cubic model with coefficients **you choose**. Write
the true coefficients down before fitting anything.

**(c)** Best subset selection over X, X², …, X¹⁰. The model picked by Cp, by BIC, and by adjusted
R²: do they agree? Report each chosen model's coefficients **next to the true ones**.

**(d)** Forward and backward stepwise. Same table. Where do they differ from (c)?

**(e)** The lasso with λ chosen by cross-validation: the CV plot, the chosen λ, and the non-zero
coefficients next to the true ones.

**(f)** New response Y = β₀ + β₇X⁷ + ε. Best subset and the lasso again. **One paragraph**: which
method found the right single term, which did not, and what that says about the two methods when
most true coefficients are zero. *Most of the credit for Q8 is here.*

## Q9 (a)–(e) · Predicting applications (`College`, `Apps`)

**(a)** Split into training and test.

**(b)** Least squares: test error.

**(c)** Ridge with λ by cross-validation: test error.

**(d)** The lasso with λ by cross-validation: test error, and the number of non-zero coefficients.

**(e)** Principal components regression with M by cross-validation: test error and the M chosen.

Finish with a **four-row table** of test errors and **two sentences**: are the four methods
really different on this data, and which would you give to the admissions office, given that
they must explain the model to a committee?
