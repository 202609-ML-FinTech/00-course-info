## Ch12 Q10 · PCA on simulated data with three groups

**(a)** Generate 60 observations in 50 variables, 20 per class, with a mean shift between the
classes. State the shift you added and why you expect PCA to see it.

**(b)** PCA, then the scatter of the first two scores coloured by class. If the classes do not
separate, go back to (a) and increase the shift; report what you changed.

**(c)** K-means with K = 3 on the raw data. Compare the clusters with the true classes in a
table. Labels are arbitrary: say how you matched them.

**(d)–(e)** K = 2 and K = 4. Describe what happened in one sentence each.

**(f)** K-means with K = 3 **on the first two score vectors only**. Compare with (c) in one
sentence.

**(g)** Scale each variable to standard deviation one and repeat (c). Then **one paragraph**:
why did scaling change the result (or not), and which version would you trust for this data?
*Most of the credit for Q10 is here.*
