## Q13 (e)–(j) · The same problem, five classifiers

Continue from HW-1102 (fit 1990–2008 on `Lag2`, test 2009–2010).

**(e)** LDA, **(f)** QDA, **(g)** KNN with K = 1, **(h)** naive Bayes: confusion matrix and test
accuracy for each.

**(i)** One table, five rows (logistic from HW-1102, LDA, QDA, KNN, NB): test accuracy,
sensitivity for "Up", specificity. Which method looks best, and **is the difference between the
top two large enough to trust** on two years of weekly data (about 104 weeks)? Give a reason.

**(j)** Experiment with predictors, transformations, interactions, and K for KNN. Report the
**best** test accuracy you found, the model that produced it, and **how many models you tried**.
Then one sentence: having tried that many, how much of the improvement would you expect to
survive on 2011–2012? *Most of the credit for (j) is in this sentence.*
