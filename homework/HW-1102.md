## Q13 (a)–(d) · Predicting the direction of the market, week by week

**(a)** Numerical and graphical summaries. Do not paste `describe()` and stop. State **one
pattern** you believe is real and **one you believe is noise**, each with the figure or number
that made you think so.

**(b)** Logistic regression of `Direction` on `Lag1`–`Lag5` and `Volume`, full data.
Which predictors are significant? For the one that is, give the odds ratio and say what it means
for a trader in one sentence.

**(c)** The confusion matrix and overall accuracy of the model in (b), **on the same data it was
fitted on**. Then answer, in words: what kinds of mistakes is the model making, and what is the
accuracy of simply predicting "Up" every week? Compare the two numbers. *This is where most of
the credit for (c) is.*

**(d)** Fit on 1990–2008 with `Lag2` only; test on 2009–2010. Confusion matrix and accuracy on
the test years. Then **one paragraph**: is this model worth trading on? Use the accuracy, the
confusion matrix, and the "always Up" baseline from (c) in your answer. A yes or no without the
three numbers earns little.
